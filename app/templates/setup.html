Teams and their working days

A team is a working week plus its holidays. Durations, lags and the workflow steps are counted in a team's working days, and a date landing on one of its days off moves to the next day it is in — so a Cairo line does not read as late over a Friday nobody worked, and neither does a Beirut one over a Sunday.
