Five things that hold everywhere

Worth knowing before anything else

  • There is one copy of everything. A deliverable's progress is the same record on the Progress tab, the Schedule and the week — change it anywhere and it changes everywhere, with nothing to keep in step.
  • Every page is live. It notices when somebody else changes something and redraws itself within a few seconds, so two people are never looking at different numbers.
  • Dates are always dd/mm/yyyy, typed or shown, whatever the machine's locale says.
  • Days are working days — durations, lags and workflow offsets are all counted on the owning team's own week and holidays.
  • Anything can be printed: the button on each tab opens the browser's print dialog, where “Save as PDF” makes the file. Charts print on their own sheet.
