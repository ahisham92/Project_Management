How to write it

One paragraph to a line. What starts the line says where it sits; the numbers are worked out for you.

# GENERALPART 1 - GENERAL
## SUMMARY1.1 SUMMARY
- textA. text
-- text1. text
--- texta. text
---- text1) text
// notea note to whoever edits it; never issued
| a | b |a table row

A line with no mark carries on the paragraph above it.

Only for some projects: start the text with a condition, and whatever sits under that paragraph goes with it.

-- {if leed=v4.1} Product Data for ...

Several conditions: {if a=x & b=y}. Either of two: {if a=x|y}. Anything but: {if a!=x}.
